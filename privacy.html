Privacy

Last updated 16 August 2026

This notice explains what information Internship Tracker handles and where it is stored.

At a glance

  • Your saved applications and notes stay in your browser.
  • Your search terms are sent to Adzuna to retrieve job listings.
  • The app does not create accounts, set advertising cookies, or run analytics.

Who is responsible

Internship Tracker is a self-hostable application and does not operate a central user-account service. The person or organisation operating the copy you use is responsible for its hosting, server logs, and support channel. A public deployment should identify its operator and provide contact details alongside this notice.

Information stored on your device

When you save or manually add an application, the app stores the record in your browser’s local storage. A record can include the role, company, location, description, job-posting URL, application status, application date, notes, source, and created or updated dates.

This information is not uploaded to the Internship Tracker server by the current application. It remains available until you remove saved applications, clear the site’s browser data, or use a browser setting that removes local storage.

Job searches and Adzuna

When you search, the app sends your keyword, selected country, optional location, and results page to the Internship Tracker server. The server forwards those search details to the Adzuna Jobs API and returns the matching listings.

Adzuna processes requests under its own privacy notice. The app does not send your saved application notes or application history to Adzuna.

Technical information

Like most web services, the hosting provider may receive technical request information such as an IP address, browser details, requested URL, and timestamps. The application code does not intentionally create a user profile from this information, but the deployment operator or hosting platform may keep security and access logs under its own retention settings.

Cookies and analytics

The current application does not set its own cookies and does not include advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics services. External job-posting websites may use their own cookies and tracking technologies after you follow a link to them.

How to delete your information

You can remove individual applications, clear all saved applications from the app, or clear this site’s stored data through your browser settings. Because saved records remain on your device, the deployment operator cannot restore them after they are deleted.

Your rights

Where data-protection law applies, you may have rights over personal information processed by the deployment operator, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Requests concerning hosting logs should be directed to the operator of the deployment you use.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
